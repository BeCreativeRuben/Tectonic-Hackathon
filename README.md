# Tectonic-Hackathon

Two moments, one advisor desk.

**In the app** is Focus Shield. Sarah’s balance is tight, an €80 purchase pauses, and she can cool off or call an advisor. Goals and the cooling-off queue live here.

**Eva’s week** is the fortnight the bank already had. She starts steady. The same two weeks tip into a tight week. Kate offers a prepared step. Eva does not want to open the app for it, and she can hold tomorrow 10:30 or Thursday 16:00. The spoken note is stored in `public/voice/eva-stress.mp3`.

**Advisor desk** keeps Sarah, Marc Janssens, and Elena from the Focus Shield file. Eva joins that queue only after her week tips. Answering her shows the talk track for the fortnight, not a paused purchase.

Marc Vermeulen on the week tape and Marc Janssens on the desk are different households.
